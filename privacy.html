Harbor Sort · Android

Privacy policy

A clear view of how your information is handled.

Effective and last updated: 20 November 2026

Godmin Games provides Harbor Sort (Android package com.harborsort.game). This policy explains how we handle information in the game, on this support page, and when you contact us.

Current testing version

Advertising and in-app purchases are disabled in the current internal-testing Android build, version 0.3.8. This policy describes that build. We will update this policy and the applicable privacy controls before enabling those services in a future release.

Information we handle

Game progress and preferences

Harbor Sort saves your level progress, coins, help charges, rewards, game preferences and other game state on your device so you can continue playing. You do not need to create a Harbor Sort account. We do not operate a server that receives your game saves.

The game also keeps a limited local diagnostic log of gameplay events to help investigate problems. This game log is not automatically uploaded to us. If you choose to send a screenshot or diagnostic information to support, that information becomes part of your support message.

Support messages

If you email godmincro@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or information that is unnecessary for your request.

Visits to this page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without signing in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or a sign-in form to this page.

Advertising and purchases

The current testing build does not request ads or initiate in-app purchases. The project includes Google AdMob, Google's consent tools and Google Play Billing for a future release, but their integration does not mean these features are active in this testing build.

When enabled in a future release, Google's advertising services can process IP-derived approximate location, device or account identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and performance diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The exact handling depends on the SDK version, age treatment, consent and settings. We will publish the applicable details and controls before activation.

Purchases, when available, will be processed by Google Play. We will not receive your full payment-card details. The game will use purchase identifiers and signed purchase information to verify and restore an entitlement, and keep a signed receipt locally. Google's purchase and payment records are handled separately from the game's local save.

Learn more in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children and families

Harbor Sort is intended for families, including children and adults. The current testing version has no in-game account registration, chat, advertising or active purchase flow. It does not ask players to provide their name, email address or date of birth to play.

A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's privacy or request deletion of information sent to support. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support for them. Advertising for children and users of unknown age will remain disabled until the appropriate age handling and advertising safeguards have been implemented and checked.

Use, sharing and security

We use local game information to provide the game, and support correspondence to respond to you. We do not sell your personal information. Support email is processed by our email provider, Google, and this page is served by our hosting provider. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by law or to address fraud, security or legal claims.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in support and security, consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. Service providers may process information outside your country under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

We limit the information we request and use HTTPS for this page. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Game saves and local diagnostics remain on your device until removed or overwritten. You can delete them using Android's app-storage controls. Clearing app storage removes progress and cannot be undone. Device or operating-system backups, if enabled, are managed separately through your device or Google account settings.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, provide follow-up support and meet applicable legal requirements. You can ask us to delete correspondence or attachments you sent. We may retain information where required by law or necessary to resolve an outstanding dispute.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, and to receive a portable copy where applicable. Contact us to make a request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that the request concerns your own data. You may also raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

We cannot remotely retrieve or erase game data that exists only on your device. Requests about Google's independently maintained account or payment records should also be directed to Google through its privacy and account controls.

Contact and policy updates

For game support or privacy questions, contact Godmin Games at godmincro@gmail.com. For a privacy request, include “Harbor Sort privacy” in the subject and tell us what you would like us to do.

We will update this page when our data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. Material changes will also be communicated in the game or through the store where appropriate.
